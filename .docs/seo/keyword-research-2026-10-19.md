# Keyword research: Power-Vac Gutter Cleaning

**Client:** Power-Vac Gutter Cleaning (Tommy Sewell), Norwich
**Site:** https://www.powervacguttercleaning.co.uk
**Date:** 19 October 2026
**Prepared by:** Build 76

## Summary

People looking for Power-Vac's services search in plain terms: "gutter cleaning" followed by a place name, the cost, or a problem they've noticed ("blocked downpipe", "moss on the roof"). We've given every page on the site one main search phrase and a few supporting ones, and rewritten the page titles, search descriptions and a few lines of copy so each page clearly answers that search.

The main phrase for the whole site is **gutter cleaning Norwich** (about 260 searches a month, including "Norwich gutter cleaning" and "gutter cleaning in Norwich"). It has very low competition (difficulty 2 out of 100), it's exactly what Power-Vac does and where, and the people searching it want to book. The site is new and doesn't rank for anything yet, so low-competition local phrases are where it can win first.

## How we chose the keywords

We pulled UK search volumes and difficulty scores from SE Ranking for 65 phrases covering every service, the towns on the areas page and the questions customers ask. We then checked who ranks on Google for "gutter cleaning Norwich" today and which phrases the strongest local competitor ranks for.

We picked phrases using four tests:

1. **Intent.** Does the person searching want to hire someone (local or commercial intent), or are they researching? Service pages get hiring phrases; news articles get questions.
2. **Local relevance.** Phrases with Norwich or Norfolk in them, or ones Google treats as local (it shows a map pack), are worth more than national volume Power-Vac can't serve.
3. **Competition.** A new site can realistically rank for phrases with a difficulty under about 15. National phrases like "gutter cleaning" (difficulty 38) are only used as supporting words.
4. **Fit.** Only phrases that describe something Power-Vac actually does, in words Tommy would use.

## What we'd do next

- **A gutter repairs page.** "Gutter repairs" is searched 2,400 times a month and "gutter repairs Norwich" 70, with low competition. Power-Vac lists minor roofline repairs but has no page for it. A short page would let the site appear for these searches.
- **A gutter cleaning cost article.** "How much does gutter cleaning cost" and its variants are the most searched questions (590 a month, difficulty 5). An article explaining what affects the price, with the £34.99 starting point, could rank well and lead to quotes.
- **Google Business Profile.** For "gutter cleaning Norwich" and "near me" searches, Google shows the map pack above the websites. Getting the profile verified, adding photos and collecting reviews will do more for these searches than any change to the website.
- **Town photos.** Before and after photos labelled with the town they were taken in.
