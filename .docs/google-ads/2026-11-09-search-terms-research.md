## Recommendation not yet built

**Ad group 3: Downpipes** → `/services/downpipe-unblocking`, keywords "blocked downpipe", "blocked gutter downpipe", "unblock downpipe", "downpipe unblocking", "blocked downpipe near me", "clogged downpipe". Google labels most of these as informational, so some searchers want to fix it themselves; the ad should lead with "we unblock it for you" and the camera, and the search terms report needs checking closely in the first fortnight.

**Optional ad group 4: Fascias and soffits** → `/services/fascia-and-soffit-cleaning`. Smaller, so it could wait until the budget allows.

At £10 a day, more ad groups split the same budget, so the gutter cleaning group stays the priority.
