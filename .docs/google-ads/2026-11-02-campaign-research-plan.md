Norfolk is about 1.4% of the UK population, so the national "gutter cleaning" and "near me" searches give roughly 200 to 250 a month in Norfolk, plus about 280 a month that name Norwich or a Norfolk town. A realistic estimate is **400 to 600 gutter cleaning searches a month in his area**, more in autumn and winter. Roof cleaning, moss and repairs add a similar amount again. Google's Keyword Planner gives exact local forecasts, but it needs his account, so that's the first job once we're linked.

### Who advertises in Norwich

On "gutter cleaning Norwich", SE Ranking has recorded ads from **Ben's Gutters** (bensgutters.com, a national gutter firm with a Norfolk page, top position June and July 2026) and, in November 2025, four roofing firms (DWS Roofing, JD Norwich Roofing, RL Roofing, JJ Norfolk Roofing) all running the same template ad ("Local Gutter Services Norwich"). It recorded no advertisers on "gutter cleaning near me", "roof repairs Norwich" or plain "gutter cleaning" for the UK. The local ad market is thin, and none of these ads mentions a camera, photos or a price. Power-Vac can stand out on the live camera, the before and after photos and "from £34.99".

### Seasonality

Gutter cleaning searches peak January to May (12,100 to 14,800 a month nationally) and stay steady through autumn. "Roof cleaning near me" and "gutter repairs near me" have risen sharply since July 2026. Starting in November is good timing: leaves are down and the winter peak follows.

### The money question

A gutter clean from £34.99 is a small job. At about £2 a click and a landing page that turns 10% of visitors into enquiries, each enquiry costs about £20. If half become jobs, each job costs about £40 in ads. That only pays if the average job is worth more than the starting price (bigger houses, downpipes, fascias added on) or customers come back every year. Roof cleaning, moss removal and repairs are worth far more per job, so they can carry higher click costs. This is why we need to know Tommy's typical job value before we set the budget.

## Proposed campaign

**One Search campaign** (not Performance Max or a Smart campaign: Search lets us control exactly which searches we pay for, which matters on a small budget).

- **Location:** people in Norwich plus a radius covering his travel area (about 25 miles, to be confirmed), set to "Presence: people in or regularly in your targeted locations", so we don't pay for people elsewhere searching about Norwich.
- **Schedule:** all week to start; review after a month, and cut hours that bring clicks but no enquiries.
- **Networks:** Google Search only. Search partners and the Display Network off.
- **Ad groups**, each sending people to the matching page:
  1. *Gutter cleaning* (gutter cleaning, gutter cleaner(s), gutter clearing, blocked gutters, gutter cleaning services, + Norwich/Norfolk variants) → the campaign landing page
  2. *Roof cleaning and moss* (roof cleaning, roof moss removal, + near me / Norwich) → roof cleaning page, or a second landing page later
  3. *Repairs* (gutter repairs, guttering repairs, roof repairs, ridge tile repointing, slipped tiles) → repairs page. Only if Tommy wants to grow repairs work: it has the highest click cost.
  4. *Downpipes and fascias* (blocked downpipe, unblock downpipe, fascia and soffit cleaning) → their service pages
- **Match types:** phrase and exact match. No broad match until conversions are being tracked properly.
- **Negative keywords:** DIY, how to, tool, vacuum for sale / hire / buy, Karcher, gutter guards, jobs, salary, course, B&Q, Screwfix, Amazon, free, plus anything irrelevant found in the search terms report each week.
- **Ads:** responsive search ads with the live camera ("Watch your gutters cleared on our camera"), before and after photos, "No ladders", "From £34.99", "Free quote", "Norwich & Norfolk". Assets: call button with his number, sitelinks to services, before and after and areas, callouts (Fully insured if true, Photos of every job, Local to Norwich), price asset, and the Business Profile linked as a location asset so his reviews and address can show.
- **Bidding:** start on Maximise clicks with a maximum CPC of about £2.50 to gather data, switch to Maximise conversions once the campaign has about 15 to 30 tracked enquiries, then consider a target cost per enquiry.
- **Conversions (B76-452):** quote form submitted (thank-you state on the landing page), calls from the ads (call asset with Google forwarding number, 30 seconds or longer), and taps on the phone number on the site. Import the Business Profile calls if available.
