# New request to Tommy: starting his Google Ads campaign

**Project:** POWER-VAC (order 162)
**Add-on:** Google Ads management (`order_services` de64e330-83dd-4b08-969b-b5a514ab50e1), so `order_service_id` is set on the request
**Subject:** We're starting your Google Ads Campaign
**Status:** draft, not sent

How access works (checked 1 November 2026 in Google's help, https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7459601): a Google Ads manager account can send a link request to a client's account using its 10-digit customer ID. The owner gets an email with an "Accept request" button, or can accept in Google Ads under Admin > Access and security > Managers, where it's listed under "Link request". Only someone with Admin access to the account can accept. The owner can remove a manager at any time from the same page. Google's help doesn't say whether the Google Ads mobile app can accept a link request, which is why the email button is the route given to Tommy.

---

Hi Tommy,

Good news: we're starting work on your Google Ads campaign.

**What we're doing first**

We're researching the best keywords for your campaign: the searches people in your area actually make when they need their gutters cleaned or a repair done, and which of those are most likely to turn into a booked job. The aim is to get the maximum return on your budget, so your money goes on the searches that bring in work, not on clicks that go nowhere.

Once that's done, we'll suggest a few budget options, each balanced to get you the best possible response for what you spend, so you can choose the one that suits you.

**Giving us access to your account**

To set up and run the campaign, we need to be added as managers on your Google Ads account. The good news is that, just like your Google Business Profile, you don't need to go through any settings yourself. We send the request from our side, and you just accept it. All we need from you is your Google Ads customer ID.

1. Open Google Ads on your phone (the app or ads.google.com) and sign in to the account you set up.
2. Look for a 10-digit number that looks like 123-456-7890, shown near your business name at the top. That's your customer ID.
3. Reply here with that number.

We'll then send you a request to link your account to ours. You'll get an email from Google with an **Accept request** button. Tap it, and if Google asks you to confirm it's you, follow the steps. That's it.

You stay the owner of the account the whole time, with full control. You can see us listed as a manager and remove us whenever you like.

**Then your campaign landing page**

Once we're in, we'll build a landing page especially for your campaign, designed to get people filling out your quote form. Rather than sending people from your ad to your homepage, it takes them straight to a page about exactly what they searched for, with a clear way to ask for a quote.

All of this is included in your service, so there's nothing extra to pay us. The only cost is the ad budget you choose, which you pay to Google directly.

Thanks,
Gavin
