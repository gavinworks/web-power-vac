**What happens next**

We've already done the keyword research: we know what people around Norwich search for when they need their gutters done, and roughly what each click costs (about £1.50 to £2.50 for gutter cleaning). Only a handful of local businesses advertise on those searches, which is good news for you. From here:

1. **Link your account.** Once you send your Google Ads customer ID (the 10-digit number at the top of your Google Ads account), we'll send the link request for you to accept. We'll also check nothing is already running that could spend your money, as Google sometimes sets up a campaign when you sign up.
2. **Budget options.** We'll use Google's own planner on your account to firm up the numbers, then send you a few budget options to choose from, each with what you can expect for the money.
3. **Tracking.** We'll set things up so we can see every call and quote request that comes from your ads, not just clicks.
4. **Build and launch.** We'll build the campaign, check it over and switch it on.
5. **Keep it working.** After that we'll keep an eye on it, cut out searches that waste money, and send you a monthly update on calls, quotes and cost.
