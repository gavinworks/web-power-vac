**Title:** Your Google ads so far, and a new article for your website

Hi Tommy,

A quick update on your Google ads, and something we'd like you to look at.

**How the ads are doing**

Your new ads have been running since Tuesday 11 November. In the first three days they've been shown 33 times and clicked twice, which has cost £2.98 so far. Both clicks came through to your website, but neither has turned into an enquiry yet.

That's too early to judge. Google spends the first week or two learning who to show the ads to, and the ads are currently spending about £1 a day of your £10, so there just hasn't been much traffic yet. We'll check again early next week. If they're still showing this little, we'll widen the searches they appear for or the area they cover, so your £400 credit gets used by Christmas.

**What we've changed**

We look at the actual searches people typed before seeing your ad. Most were right, like "gutter cleaner" and "roof cleaning", but a few were people looking for tools to do it themselves, like an extendable gutter pole or a moss scraper, or asking "how do you clean moss off roof tiles". We've stopped your ads showing for those, so you don't pay for clicks from people who were never going to book you.

**Why we've written an article**

Those moss searches gave us an idea. Quite a lot of people search for things like "moss on roof" and "roof moss treatment", and at the moment your website doesn't show up for any of them. People who read how to clear moss themselves often realise it means climbing onto the roof and decide to get someone in. So we've written an article for your website about moss on roofs: whether it does any harm, when it's worth clearing, how to do it safely, and why it's often a job for you. It links to your roof cleaning page.

You can read it exactly as it will look on your website here: https://www.powervacguttercleaning.co.uk/preview/news/moss-on-your-roof-should-you-remove-it?share=ba3b5f74-d731-4a9e-b689-e67f20a0fbbb

It isn't live yet, and only people with this link can see it. Could you check two things for us?

1. Is everything in it right? In particular, we say you clear moss by hand and vacuum the gutters out afterwards.
2. Do you ever treat roofs with a moss-killing spray after cleaning them? If you do, we'll mention it in the article and add "roof moss treatment" to your ads, because a lot of people search for it.

If you're happy with it, we'll publish it and share it as a post on your Google Business Profile too, so it reaches people there as well.

Writing articles like this is normally part of the SEO work we do for clients, but we're keen to see you get off to a good start, so this one's on us.
