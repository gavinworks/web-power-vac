# Campaign landing page: research brief

**Client:** Power-Vac Gutter Cleaning (Tommy Sewell, order 162)
**Date:** 2 November 2026
**Linear:** B76-451 (campaign landing page), under B76-302
**Goal of the page:** turn a click on a "gutter cleaning Norwich" ad into a phone call or a quote request.

Every design decision below comes from one of two pieces of research done on 2 November 2026: what makes paid-search landing pages convert in general, and who Tommy's customers are and how they behave. Evidence strength is marked: **[Google]** Google's own documentation or UK law, **[Data]** large datasets (correlational), **[Test]** controlled A/B tests (usually one company, often another sector), **[Survey]** what people say they do, **[Reviews]** mined from real customer reviews, **[Opinion]** practitioner advice. Weak evidence is used only where nothing better exists, and says so.

## Who we're talking to

**Most buyers are older homeowners in the suburbs and villages, not the city.** Norwich city has a median age of 34, only 42% of households own their home, and only 4.5% of homes are bungalows. In Broadland (the ring around the city: Taverham, Drayton, Hellesdon, Sprowston) the median age is 48, 26% are 65 or over, 77% own their home, 30% of homes are bungalows and 28% are detached. South Norfolk is similar (24% bungalows, 31% detached, 73% owners). Norfolk as a whole has 23% bungalows against 9% for England. [Google: ONS Census 2021; VOA council tax stock, March 2026]

**Not climbing a ladder is a real reason to book.** Ladder falls send about 7,674 people to hospital in England each year; the average patient is 58, most are men aged 45 to 74, and 90% are emergencies. [Data: NHS admissions analysed by the Ladder Association, 2022] The buyer is often the person who used to do it himself, or whose family has told him to stop.

**They're online but cautious.** Adults aged 55 to 74 are the best at spotting paid search results, and people over 75 are the most likely to ignore anything that looks suspicious. [Survey: Ofcom Adults' Media Use and Attitudes 2026, 7,533 adults] Tradespeople get a lot of phone calls from local-services searchers. [Survey: Google/Ipsos 2013, dated] We have no current UK figure showing older people prefer calling, so the page makes calling and the form equally easy.

**What they're afraid of.** 58% of people who struggled to choose a trader "couldn't find a trader they trusted". Among unhappy customers, 27% felt overcharged and 23% said the job wasn't done as agreed. [Survey: Which?, 16,000+, 2023] Trading Standards has warned about cold callers offering cheap gutter cleaning where "no work is actually being done". [Official: Wrexham Trading Standards warning] Earlier Which? research found 62% worried the trader wouldn't turn up on time.

**What they praise and complain about** in 15,000 reviews of GutterPRO, Ben's Gutters and Gutter Force. [Reviews]
- Praised: "arrived exactly on time having texted me en route", "showed me photos of the inside of the gutters before and after cleaning", "clean and tidy with no mess", "great value for money", "no pushy sales pitch", "at short notice".
- Complaints: "an hour late", "after photos, not received anything", "dirty drip marks were left on the fascias", "damaged plants", "took money upfront", "Misleading, NOT a local company".

**Tommy's own customers say the same things.** His TrustATrader profile (checked 2 November 2026) has 6 reviews averaging 5.0, rated 5 for punctuality, cleanliness, quality and value. They say "on time, professional and great value for money" (Kelly, 19 Oct 2026), "explained everything in great detail which gave me great piece mind" (sic, Madison Ward, 1 Oct 2026), "always on time, excellent outcome" (Romany Stewart, 16 Sep 2026) and "fantastic price and work carried out" (Becki Day, 18 May 2026). TrustATrader has verified his identity, address, customer references (22 Oct 2026) and insurance (Simply Business, 21 Oct 2026), and records 0 missed appointments in 6 months. His Google profile has only 1 review, which doesn't describe a job.

**Competitors leave clear gaps.** No Norwich competitor shows a price above the fold, none lets you watch the camera live, Gutter Force's newest testimonial is from 2014, and the Ben's Gutters and guttercleanernorfolk.co.uk pages are national templates whose image captions name Bristol streets. [Direct observation, 2 November 2026]

## What converts

1. **The headline repeats the ad.** Google ranks and prices ads partly on landing page experience: relevance to the ad and keywords, the same call to action as the ad, speed on mobile, simple navigation, easy contact and clarity about the service. [Google: Ad Rank, Quality Score and landing page help, 2026]
2. **Tap to call, big and always there on a phone.** [Survey: Google/Ipsos] The sticky bar has no published test behind it. [Opinion]
3. **Fast on mobile.** On lead generation sites, 0.1s faster on mobile went with 21.6% more people finishing a form. [Data: Deloitte for Google, 37 brands, 2020]
4. **Proof near the top and beside the form**, with the rating, the number of reviews and recent, dated, named reviews. 68% only use businesses rated 4 stars or more, 74% look for reviews from the last three months, and 47% won't use a business with fewer than 20 reviews. [Survey: BrightLocal 2026, US] Buying likelihood peaks at 4.2 to 4.5 stars, not a perfect 5. [Data: Spiegel Research Center]
5. **A short form.** Cutting a form from 11 to 4 fields raised conversions by 120%, and adding a phone field cut one form's conversions by 36%. [Test, older and other sectors] Multi-step forms have no reliable evidence either way.
6. **No menu, one goal.** Removing navigation raised conversions by 16 to 28% on HubSpot's middle-funnel pages and doubled them at Yuppiechef. [Test, single companies]
7. **Real photos, not stock.** Eye-tracking shows stock people are ignored as filler, while real staff and real product photos are studied. [Test: NN/g eye-tracking] 69% of homeowners "would think twice" about a business using stock images. [Survey: Scorpion, vendor]
8. **Plain, short copy.** Pages written at a 5th to 7th grade reading level converted at a median of 11.1%, against 5.3% for professional-level copy. Home services pages did best at about 300 words. [Data: Unbounce, 41,000 pages, 2024]
9. **Quick replies.** Firms that replied within an hour were about 7 times more likely to qualify a lead. [Test: HBR audit of 2,241 firms] 88% of homeowners expect a reply within 24 hours. [Survey: Scorpion]
10. **The price on show.** There's no home-services test for showing a price, but the ad shows "from £34.99" and no competitor does. [Opinion plus logic]

**What the page must not do:** fake urgency ("only 2 slots left", countdown timers), invented reviews, or a badge for a scheme he isn't in. All three are banned outright under Schedule 20 of the DMCC Act 2024, and the CMA enforces it. [Google: UK law]

**Benchmark to beat:** about 8% of ad clicks becoming a call or form (LocaliQ 2026, home and home improvement, US). The top quarter of home improvement pages convert at 19.5% or more (Unbounce).

## The page, decision by decision

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| URL `/gutter-cleaning-norwich`, `noindex`, no menu: logo and phone number only | Message match [Google]; no navigation [Test]; noindex so it doesn't compete with the homepage in search |
| H1 repeats the ad: "Gutter cleaning in Norwich, from £34.99" | Message match [Google]; price is a clear gap against competitors |
| Under it: "Watch your gutters on our camera while we clean them, all from the ground. No ladders." | Answers "was it actually done?" [Survey: Which?; Reviews]; no-ladder reason to book [Data] |
| Proof straight under the headline: "5.0 from 6 reviews on TrustATrader · Insurance checked · 0 missed appointments" | Rating and count near the top [Survey: BrightLocal]; real, verified, not a homemade badge [Google: DMCC] |
| Two equal buttons: "Call Tommy" (tap to call) and "Get a free quote" (jumps to the form). Sticky call bar on phones | Click to call [Survey]; two kinds of buyer, urgent and planned [Reviews] |
| Hero image: a real Power-Vac job (the camera screen or a before and after on a Norfolk bungalow) | Real images [Test: NN/g]; bungalows and detached homes are the market [Google: VOA] |
| Short "How it works" in 3 steps: quote, we clean from the ground while you watch, you get the before and after photos | Plain, short copy [Data]; answers the job-not-done fear [Survey] |
| A before and after slider of his own photos | Proof of work [Reviews: "showed me photos of the inside of the gutters"] |
| Three TrustATrader reviews, named and dated, next to the form | Recent named reviews beside the form [Survey]; Tommy's real customers |
| "What you can count on" promises, only ones Tommy confirms | Answers top fears: no-shows, overcharging, mess, cowboys [Survey; Reviews] |
| Form: name, phone or email (one field), postcode, optional "anything we should know?" and service tick boxes, plus "We'll reply the same day" if Tommy can keep to it | Short form [Test]; reply speed [Test: HBR] |
| A Tommy section: his photo and "a local sole trader, not a national call centre" | Real staff photos [Test: NN/g]; "NOT a local company" complaint [Reviews] |
| Areas line naming Taverham, Drayton, Hellesdon, Sprowston, Wymondham and others | Where the buyers are [Google: ONS]; message match for town searches |
| Three short FAQs: price, do I need to be home, how long does it take | Common objections, kept short [Data: copy length] |
| About 300 to 400 words in all, short sentences | [Data: Unbounce] |
| No pop-ups, no chat widget, no animation that hides content before it loads | Speed [Data]; older, cautious visitors [Survey: Ofcom] |

## Facts to confirm with Tommy before launch

The page only says what's true. These are either unconfirmed or need his OK:

1. **Which phone number the ads should use.** The site uses 01603 339225; TrustATrader shows 07463 919347. A landline looks more local and established; a mobile is what he answers on the job.
2. **Reply time.** Can he reply to every quote request the same day?
3. **On-the-way text.** Does he text before arriving? Customers praise it and complain when it's missing.
4. **Payment.** Is it paid after the job, never upfront? "Took money upfront" is a common complaint.
5. **Photos.** Does every customer get their before and after photos the same day, and how (text, WhatsApp, email)?
6. **Do they need to be home?** And can they watch the camera if they are?
7. **"Over 20 years' experience"**, as his TrustATrader profile says. Is that accurate, and in what?
8. **A photo of Tommy** with his van or the SkyVac, and a few more before and after photos from bungalows and houses around Norwich.
9. **Permission to quote his TrustATrader reviews** (first names and dates only), and his OK to show the TrustATrader rating.
10. **Google reviews.** With 1 review on Google, asking his customers for Google reviews will do more for the ads than anything on the page. Ads can show a seller rating, and the map pack is ranked on reviews.

## Built (2 November 2026)

The page is at `/gutter-cleaning-norwich` on mock data (`src/lib/mock-data.ts`), until Tommy approves it. It then moves to a `campaign_pages` collection in Directus. What changed alongside it:

- The quote form is now its own component (`QuoteForm` in `src/components/site/quote-section.tsx`). The campaign page uses it without the email field and only offers gutter cleaning and its add-ons. The rest of the site is unchanged.
- Email is optional in `/api/quote`, and the `submissions.email` field in Directus is no longer required (empty saves as null). The "[Website] New Quote Request" flow now says "Not given, so give them a ring" when there's no email, drops the "Reply by email" button, and sets the reply-to to info@ instead.
- The quote form hasn't been test-submitted, because a test emails Tommy. Send one from the page once Gavin's happy, with "TEST" in the name, and check the email that arrives.

**Wording on the page that Tommy must confirm before launch** (on top of the list above):

- "We'll give you a fixed price before we start" (step 1 and the first FAQ)
- "The vacuum pole reaches up to three storeys" (step 2; his own photos show three-storey houses)
- "Do I need to be at home? No, as long as we can get to the gutters"
- "Most houses take under an hour"
- The before and after slider uses the example photos, labelled "Example photos". His own camera stills from a real job should replace them.
- The camera screen in the hero is the same stock still the homepage uses.

## Measuring it

- Conversions: form submitted, tap on the phone number, calls from the ad's call button (30 seconds or more). See B76-452.
- Target: 8% or better of ad clicks becoming a call or form in the first month, then test changes one at a time (headline, hero image, form layout) once there's enough traffic. At about 100 to 150 clicks a month, each test needs a month or two to show anything, so only test big changes.
