# PostHog analytics (B76-515)

**Date:** 11 November 2026
**Where:** PostHog EU, organisation "Power Vac Gutter Cleaning" (one organisation per client, free plan), project "Power-Vac website" (id 344619), time zone Europe/London. Cookieless tracking switched on in the project (stateful server hash). IP addresses anonymised.

## How it works on the site

- `src/lib/analytics.ts` starts PostHog from the cookie banner on every page except Directus previews and prototypes.
- **Before a choice, or after No thanks:** cookieless mode. Nothing is stored on the device (checked 11 November 2026: no cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage). Visitors are told apart by a daily hash on PostHog's servers, so visits and page views are counted for everyone. No session replay.
- **After Accept:** PostHog sets its cookie and starts session replay with every input masked. The Google tag loads as before.
- **Withdrawing** ("Cookie settings" in the footer, then No thanks): replay stops, PostHog's cookie and data are cleared, and the Google cookies and `_gcl` localStorage entries are cleared. Only the two "remember my choice" entries stay.
- Events: `$pageview`, autocapture clicks, heatmaps, web vitals, plus `quote_form_sent` (page, services ticked) and `phone_tapped` (page).
- Requests go through `/ingest` on the site's own domain (rewrites in `next.config.ts`) so ad blockers don't drop them.
- Test events from localhost on 11 November 2026 are in the project; filter them out with `$host` = `www.powervacguttercleaning.co.uk`.

## Cookies page wording (publish in Directus at the same time as the deploy)

> **Cookies on this site**
>
> We only use cookies if you say yes.
>
> Without cookies, we still count visits in a way that stores nothing on your device. Our analytics service, PostHog, works out a temporary, anonymous code on its own servers so we can see how many people visit and which pages they look at. It can't tell who you are.
>
> If you accept, we also use the cookies below. Google Ads tells us when someone who clicked one of our adverts gets in touch, so we stop paying for adverts that don't work. PostHog lets us see how people use the site across visits and record visits so we can improve the pages. Anything you type is hidden in those recordings. We don't use any of this to show you adverts elsewhere.
>
> | Cookie | Set by | What it's for | How long it lasts |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | `_gcl_au` | Google Ads | Links an enquiry to the advert that brought you here | 90 days |
> | `ph_…_posthog` | PostHog | Recognises your visits so we can see how the site is used, and records visits | 1 year |
>
> We also remember your choice in your browser's storage, so we don't ask again on every page.
>
> **Changing your mind**
>
> Use "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page to change your choice at any time. If you change to "No thanks", we delete these cookies. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser's settings.
