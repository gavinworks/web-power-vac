## Share links for draft articles

Clients can now read a draft article on the real site without it going live: `scripts/share-article.mjs <slug>` creates a Directus share for the article and prints `/preview/news/<slug>?share=<id>`. The share can read only that article (drafts included); the rest of the page uses published content. The link expires after 14 days by default and stops working as soon as the share is deleted. Tested on Directus 12.6.1 before building, and locally after (share link 200 and noindex; other article, made-up id and deleted share all 404).
