## Gutter vacuum cleaning page

## Photo galleries on service pages

- Every service now has three optional fields in Directus, under the page text: Gallery heading, Gallery description and Gallery (photos). The section is hidden when there are no photos.
- Each photo's Title in Directus is its alt text for screen readers, so it should describe what the photo shows.
- The photos are stored through a new `services_gallery` collection. The Public and Website preview policies can read it.
- The Editors policy has no access to `services_gallery` yet, so Tommy can't add or reorder gallery photos until it's given create, read, update and delete on that collection (the same as on `services`).
